Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What types of cleaning do you offer?
A full range across three areas: commercial (offices, schools, retail, medical), domestic and end-of-tenancy, and specialist work including builders cleans, window and carpet cleaning, biohazard decontamination and mould removal.
Are you fully insured and accredited?
Yes. We're fully insured for every service we provide and work to recognised industry and ISO standards, with health, safety and compliance built into how we operate.
Are your staff trained and vetted?
All operatives are trained in-house to consistent standards, with regular audits by our supervisors. Where a contract requires it, we supply staff who have passed advanced DBS checks.
Do you offer bespoke schedules?
Yes. Early mornings, evenings, weekends or out-of-hours — we build the schedule around your premises to keep disruption to a minimum.
What areas do you cover?
We cover [your towns and surrounding areas]. Not sure if you're in range? Get in touch and we'll confirm straight away.
How do we arrange a site visit?
Call, email or use the form below. We'll arrange a convenient time to assess the space and provide a tailored, no-obligation quote.
